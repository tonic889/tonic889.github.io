Don't take our word for it

On Android, Green Prophet doesn't request internet permission at all — so the operating system itself stops it from making any network connection. It physically cannot send your data anywhere.

Want to check? Turn on airplane mode and use the app. Everything works exactly the same, because it never needed a connection in the first place. No telemetry, no analytics, no account, no servers.

What it is — and isn't

Green Prophet answers one question: how much money should be in my account a week, a month, or a year from now? That's it.

It's not a full-featured budgeting app. It doesn't keep a history of the past, sync with your bank, do budgeting, or make investment suggestions. There are lots of (elaborate, subscription-required) apps that already do that, and I'm not interested in replicating them.

Why I made this

It started as a project for myself. I couldn't find anything that did this, the way I wanted it, for free. So I wrote it myself. Eventually I figured I'd come far enough that I should just publish it.

I genuinely don't know if anyone else will find it useful. This isn't my million-dollar idea and I'm not raising venture capital for it. Lol. If nothing else, it's a good line on my resume.

But if "how much will be in my account next month?" is a question you've ever asked yourself — maybe it'll be useful to you too.
