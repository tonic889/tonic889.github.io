Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use Green Prophet?

No. Green Prophet is offline-first and has no sign-up. Your financial data lives on your device, not on any server.

Do you connect to my bank?

No. There are no bank logins or linked accounts. You enter and manage your data yourself, so your credentials are never involved.

Is there a subscription or any tracking?

No subscription and no telemetry. I don't track usage or collect analytics. On Android, the app doesn't even request internet permission — you can verify it works in airplane mode. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

How do I delete my data?

Because everything is stored on your device, you can delete all your data anytime by tapping Clear Transactions on the Settings tab. Removing the app also deletes its local data.

Which platforms are supported?

Green Prophet is built for iOS, Android, and Windows. Availability on each store may roll out at slightly different times — check the download links on the Green Prophet page for what's live right now.
