Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to use Green Prophet?

No. Green Prophet is offline-first and has no sign-up. Your financial data lives on your device, not on our servers.

Do you connect to my bank?

No. There are no bank logins or linked accounts. You enter and manage your data yourself, so your credentials are never involved.

Is there a subscription or any tracking?

No subscription and no telemetry. We don't track usage or collect analytics. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.

How do I delete my data?

Because everything is stored on your device, all transactions can be deleted by tapping the "Clear Transactions" on the Settings tab. Alternatively, removing the app deletes its local data.

Which platforms are supported?

Green Prophet is built for iOS, Android, and Windows.
