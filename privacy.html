Dungeon East

Privacy Policy

This policy applies to the Dungeon East website and to all Dungeon East apps for iPhone, Android, and Windows, including Green Prophet. Where an individual app differs from this policy, that difference is noted explicitly below.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal information. Our apps are designed to work without accounts and without sending your data off your device.

Operating-system diagnostics

Apple, Google, and Microsoft each offer an optional, OS-level setting that lets you share anonymized crash and usage diagnostics with app developers (for example, Apple's "Share With App Developers" setting). This sharing is controlled by you in your device settings, is collected and anonymized by the platform — not by us — and is governed by that platform's own privacy policy. We receive only aggregated, anonymized reports through these channels, if any, and never personally identifiable information.

Ads and analytics

Our apps contain no advertising and no third-party analytics. Green Prophet, specifically, makes no network connections at all and on Android does not request internet permission.

Business changes

Because we do not collect or hold any user data, there is no user data to sell, share, or transfer — including in the event of a sale, merger, or partnership. Any future change to our practices will be reflected on this page before it takes effect, and will never apply retroactively to data we never collected.

Your consent

By using our site or apps, you consent to this privacy policy as it may be updated from time to time.

Changes to this policy

If we change this privacy policy, we will post the changes on this page.

Summary of changes so far

  • March 24, 2026: Updated for Green Prophet on iOS, Android, and Windows; added Application Insights telemetry disclosure.
